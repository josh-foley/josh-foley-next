I've started and abandoned a personal blog at least four times. Each
attempt collapsed under the weight of its own setup: a custom static
generator, an over-designed theme, a CMS I never logged into.

This time I'm trying something different. The whole stack is boring on
purpose:

- **Next.js 15** with the App Router for routing and static generation
- **Velite** to parse MDX with a typed schema
- **Tailwind** for styling, with the typography plugin doing most of the
  heavy lifting on prose
- **next-themes** for a single light/dark toggle

That's it. No newsletter integration, no analytics, no comment system.
If the only way to make the writing happen is to delete every excuse,
that's what I'll do.

## What I want to write about

Mostly software — the things I notice while shipping, the corners of
languages and tools that turn out to be more interesting than they look.
Occasionally something else.

If any of that sounds interesting, the [blog index](/blog) is the place
to start.
